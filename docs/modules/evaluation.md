# evaluation

- **Responsibility:** deterministic point-in-time ENTRY valuation for the first `1h` signal outcome and a one-family evidence report.
- **Owned data:** `evaluation.evaluation_runs`, `evaluation.entry_outcomes` and `evaluation.evaluation_reports`. Runs own complete provenance; outcomes retain selected market observation identity/source/quality and exact returns; reports own ordered content and fingerprints.
- **Public API:** `EvaluationApi` accepts an accepted signal ID, the `1h` horizon and complete run provenance, and returns an immutable priced or explicitly unpriced outcome with aggregate counts and report fingerprint.
- **Allowed dependencies:** `kernel::api`, `marketdata::api`, `signal::api`.
- **Transactions:** signal and point-in-time market projections are read through module APIs before one short evaluation-owned transaction atomically inserts or resolves the run, outcome and report. No evaluation SQL reads another module's tables.
- **Valuation contract:** entry is the earliest observation strictly after signal availability and strictly before the one-hour horizon boundary; without such an entry the outcome is explicitly unpriced. The horizon observation is the earliest deterministic eligible observation at or after one hour and no later than the evaluation cutoff. Gross, friction and net returns use scale 8 and `HALF_EVEN`. Round-trip friction is `0.05000000` below USD 50,000 liquidity, `0.04000000` from USD 50,000 to below 200,000, and `0.03000000` from USD 200,000.
- **Reproducibility:** the evaluation cutoff is normalized to microseconds before queries, identity, provenance publication and persistence. Mandatory build/source/dirty-state, algorithm, configuration, dataset and cutoff provenance participates in length-prefixed SHA-256 identities and report content. This deterministic slice has no seed. Equal retries return identical evidence; immutable conflicts fail.
- **Invariants:** missing entry or horizon evidence creates a counted `UNPRICED` row; incomplete provenance fails before publication; evaluation consumes the immutable signal snapshot and never recalculates later risk or wallet state.
- **Non-goals:** additional horizons, avoidance/shadow outcomes, virtual positions, lifecycle exits, portfolio statistics, profitability claims and paper/live execution.
- **Main tests:** strict post-decision entry, deterministic horizon selection, all friction tiers, exact USD 1.00 to USD 1.20 returns (`0.20000000`, `0.04000000`, `0.16000000`), unpriced outcomes, provenance rejection, retry determinism and PostgreSQL V4 ownership/constraints.
