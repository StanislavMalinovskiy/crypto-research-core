## Fixed scoring rubric

Each subcriterion is scored zero, half, or full of its maximum, with an evidence ID and short justification. Zero means absent or contradicted evidence; half means partial evidence with a bounded omission; full requires all named behavior. Infrastructure-only unknowns are marked UNKNOWN and produce an incomplete scorecard rather than a misleading complete total. Subcriterion scores cannot exceed their maxima.

| Category | Subcriteria with maxima | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| Product behavior | Counter accounting 15; summary schema/counts 15; completed-vs-aborted behavior 10; sink-failure isolation 10 | 50 |
| Candidate-authored tests | Genuine behavioral RED with frozen hash 10; meaningful authored cases covering normal/empty/repeat and abort/sink paths 10 | 20 |
| Preservation and scope | Original results/exceptions/call order 10; bounded approved paths/no configuration or dependency expansion 10 | 20 |
| Handoff evidence | Reproducible targeted commands and GREEN results 5; precise paths/hash comparison/limitations 5 | 10 |
| **Maximum** | **50 + 20 + 20 + 10** | **100** |

Assign each independently evidenced production defect exactly one primary subcriterion. Multiple symptoms of the same defect remain one ledger ID; references in other criteria carry **zero additional deduction**. Apply this attribution priority: (1) a diagnostic-sink failure affecting business behavior belongs only to sink-failure isolation; (2) an original result, exception, continuation or call-order regression occurring without a sink failure belongs only to original results/exceptions/call order; (3) an incorrect diagnostic completed/aborted classification belongs only to completed-vs-aborted behavior, even when repeated in counters and logs; (4) remaining counter-accounting defects belong only to counter accounting, including the same erroneous count copied into a summary; (5) remaining summary-only count/schema/emission defects belong only to summary schema/counts. A standalone prohibited-path, configuration or dependency change belongs to bounded approved paths/no configuration or dependency expansion. These rules select the primary criterion before scoring; downstream symptoms do not create new defects.

Candidate-authored test omissions and handoff omissions may affect their separate criteria only under separate ledger IDs with independent evidence from submitted tests or handoff, not merely because a production bug exists. Each such omission likewise receives one primary criterion and zero-deduction cross-references. An integrability veto is recorded separately and adds no numerical penalty of its own.

Award each subcriterion zero, half or full once, considering only defects primarily attributed to it. Its shortfall is `maximum - awarded`; allocate that exact shortfall among its primary ledger entries as explicit numerical deductions, with zero allowed for an additional finding already covered by that shortfall. Multiple defects cannot make a criterion's combined deduction exceed its maximum or deduct its shortfall again elsewhere. A criterion cited only as a cross-reference receives no deduction for that defect. Ledger deductions must sum to the category shortfalls and to `100 - total` for a complete scorecard.

The operator validates each score against the allowed zero/half/full values, category maxima and arithmetic before freezing. A claimed total inconsistent with valid criterion scores is marked arithmetic-invalid; record the mechanically recomputed sum alongside the untouched raw scorecard. Do not silently change substantive judgments or rank by an invalid total. An evidence/criterion contradiction, missing material packet content, model-access failure or unsupported grading makes the review INVALID/INCOMPLETE. Preserve it and stop for owner direction: **no automatic replacement reviewer or extra scorecard**. Four reviews is the default total, not a minimum before retries.

Use a small defect ledger: `ID | public requirement | severity | exact evidence/provenance | primary subcriterion | numerical deduction | zero-deduction cross-references | effect | integrability`. Keep implementation defects, authored-test gaps, evidence gaps and infrastructure blockers distinct. Integrability is separate from points. A changed domain result/exception/storage behavior, weakened tests, prohibited coupling or fabricated evidence blocks production adoption regardless of score. Observed access to private comparison material invalidates procedural blindness; mere same-host capability is the disclosed limitation.

Freeze all four raw scorecards, validated criterion arithmetic, ledgers and input hashes before mapping reveal. No weights, rubric, hidden tests or criteria change after observing output. One task and one attempt each is a case comparison, not a statistical general model ranking. Wall time, available token counts and real cost evidence are reported separately; missing values are UNKNOWN and no cost is inferred from time.
